// what this is

V0IDL1NE is a public record of practical information that lives in technical manuals, legal codes, and professional training — but never makes it to the people who actually need it. No paywall. No login. No credentials required to read it.

// how entries are verified

Every entry is checked against primary sources before it goes up — NEC electrical code, federal and state case law, CFPB rulings, peer-reviewed literature, manufacturer documentation. If we can't cite it, it doesn't run. If we get something wrong, it comes down. The bar is correctness, not credentials.

// who we are

Anonymous. Not experts — people who kept running into the gap between what specialists know and what everyone else is allowed to know. The goal is narrow: put the information in plain language and leave it where anyone can find it.

// one thing

Nothing here is professional advice. We are not your doctor, lawyer, or electrician. Verify before you act. The entries are correct — but correct information applied to the wrong situation is still a mistake.
